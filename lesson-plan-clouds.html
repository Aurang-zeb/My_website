Lesson Plan: Clouds & Flight

This lesson plan was used during the short internship to guide a classroom activity about clouds, birds, and weather.

Learning Objectives

  • Understand how different cloud types affect flight.
  • Describe how weather conditions change bird movement.
  • Use a simple simulator to explore scientific concepts.

Lesson Activities

  • Introduction with real-world examples and pictures.
  • Play the bird life simulator app and make observations.
  • Answer short quiz questions about clouds and flight.
  • Reflect on the activity and share key findings.
